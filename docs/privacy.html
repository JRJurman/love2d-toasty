Privacy Policy for Toasty

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Toasty does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connections of any kind.

Information We Collect

Toasty does not collect, share, or sell any data.
  • Toasty does not create accounts or require you to sign in.
  • Toasty does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact information.
  • Toasty does not include analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services.
  • Toasty does not show advertising or work with advertising networks.
  • Toasty does not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • Toasty does not make network requests.

Data Stored on Your Device

Toasty saves your preferences in a single settings file on your own device, so the game remembers various settings. That file holds your audio volume levels, cursor color, animation speed, and accessibility options such as text-to-speech, auto-advance, and single-tap mode.

This file never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and the developers of Toasty have no access to it. You can erase it at any time by choosing Reset Settings in the game's options menu, or by uninstalling the game.

Screen Reader and Text-to-Speech

Toasty is designed to be played with a screen reader. When speech is turned on, the game hands the text it wants spoken — card names, menu labels, and the state of the game — to the screen reader or text-to-speech service built into your device. That text describes the game only. It is spoken and discarded: never recorded, never stored, and never sent over a network.

Your speech and screen reader settings are controlled by you through your operating system, not by Toasty.

Third-Party Platforms

Toasty is distributed through the Apple App Store, Google Play, and itch.io. These platforms operate independently and may collect their own information when you download, install, or update the game — e.g. download counts, or crash reports you have chosen to share with them. That information is governed by their privacy policies. The developers of Toasty receive only anonymous, aggregated summaries from them, such as total download counts.

Children's Privacy

Toasty collects no personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Nothing in the game asks for, transmits, or stores personal data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date above.
