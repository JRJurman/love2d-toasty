A push-your-luck deck builder to build large stacks of toast!

You're the new chef at the world famous Toast-only restaurant! We've got all the ingredients ready to roll, but be careful, we can't be caught serving no sandwiches - those are forbidden here!

Toasty is a push-your-luck deck building game. During a shift, you'll draw ingredient cards to place on bread. Every stack needs bread to get started, but if you draw a bread card when you already have a stack going, you'll end up making a sandwich! Between rounds you'll have a chance to get new cards to add to your deck.

Toasty is blind and low-vision friendly, made as part of the Games for Blind Gamers game jam. It works with built-in Text to Speech Systems and Screen Readers. You can use swipe gestures and double tap to navigate and interact with elements in the game.
